# Curadh Cooking

A profile-first cooking and recipe-discovery project for people whose real food needs do not fit one rigid diet label.

The product is intended to answer practical questions first:

- What can I cook?
- What ingredients do I have?
- How does this recipe fit this person's preferences and sensitivities?
- What can I substitute?
- What tools do I need?
- How much time will it take?
- What nutritional information matters here?

Named approaches such as low-histamine, low-FODMAP, vegan, gluten-free, or POTS-oriented nutrition may provide useful starting mappings, but they are not treated as identities or absolute rules. Individual profile choices and ingredient-level overrides take precedence.

## Product principles

- **Profile first, label second.** A named mapping supplies defaults; a person's explicit settings win.
- **Explain, do not decree.** Recipe fit should show why an ingredient was flagged rather than collapse everything into a mysterious safe/unsafe score.
- **Food first.** Recipe pages foreground ingredients, quantities, method, tools, time, yield, nutrition, substitutions, sensitivity notes, and source.
- **No lifestyle-blog filler.** External recipe discovery should extract the useful cooking information while preserving attribution and links.
- **Uncertainty is data.** Mappings may be incomplete, conditional, disputed, or source-dependent; provenance and confidence belong in the model.
- **Privacy by default.** Real household health information and personal profiles do not belong in the repository.
