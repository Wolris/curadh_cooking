# Recipe 0001 — Cook Run Evidence — V3 Success

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Recipe:** Oatmeal Sandwich Bread  
**Outcome:** Successful  
**Promotion result:** Became the first canonical Curadh Cooking recipe

## Intent

Produce a wheat-free sandwich/toast bread that is:

- soft/fluffy relative to prior GF loaves;
- between moist and dry, leaning dry;
- flexible rather than crumbly;
- lighter / less dense;
- neutral/savory;
- soft-crusted.

## Formula

See the canonical recipe. No extra milk or flour was added during the run.

## Actual equipment / settings

- Breadman bread machine
- White program
- Light crust
- 1.5 lb loaf setting

The 1.5 lb setting was initially an accidental default compared with earlier 2 lb attempts, but the result was successful and is therefore preserved as actual evidence rather than "corrected" after the fact.

## Observations during cooking

- Batter was significantly thicker than the remembered prior loaf.
- At the first Breadman rest, the mixer blade was removed.
- The blade left a visible cavity because the batter was thick enough not to flow closed.
- Batter was gently redistributed/smoothed with a rubber spatula.
- The Breadman later performed another spin/punch/rest stage with no blade installed.
- No extra milk was added after rise had begun.
- No supplemental oven baking was needed.
- A butter knife inserted near the end came out without stuck batter, with only slight moisture.

## Crust finish

- About 2 Tbsp butter
- Microwaved 20 seconds at 50% power
- Applied warm to all six sides

## Cooling

- Cooled on a wire rack in normal loaf orientation.
- Sliced only after the loaf had cooled fully.

## Result markers

### Moisture
**Hit the requested target.**

### Flexibility
**Hit the requested target.**

### Density / lightness
**Hit the requested target.** Significantly lighter and less dense than the earlier GF loaves.

### Flavor
**Hit the requested target.** Described as delicious.

### Height / scale
**Improvement opportunity.** Approximately half the height of the earlier GF loaves.

The shorter loaf does not make the run unsuccessful. The other eating-quality markers more than compensated for the scale difference.

## Product lesson

A cooking result needs to preserve both:

1. the recipe/formula being attempted; and
2. what actually happened during one specific run.

This run proves why Curadh Cooking should not treat "recipe version" and "cooking attempt/result" as the same object.
